## Fixed after this campaign

A test outside the bead's changed files is now flaky when the run already found it flaky, when an open
alloy-bug names it as flaky (`alloy_flaky` metadata, or a "Flaky test <key>" title), or when one of up to two
reruns passes. The landing gate and the check loop share this rule (`known_red.find_flaky`).

n = 3. The done-rate and needs-human changes here are the clearest results of the series. The oracle gain over
0a80098 (0.890 against 0.864) is within the run-to-run spread.
